**For:** the owner, to sign off or amend.

**Status:** **approved in full by the owner on 25 September 2026, and shipped.** Every Variant B row below is live, including the practitioner-band replacement (Section 2, note). "Two working days" is kept on the For Organisations form.

## Why this exists

`COPY_DECK.md` §11 recommends Variant A for the home page and Variant B — Momentum — "where the reader is choosing to be moved: the Academy pages, the Careers page, LinkedIn, and any campaign landing page." The home page ships A. The Academy and Careers surfaces have never been rewritten in B; they still carry the copy the old site had. This is that rewrite, offered for sign-off.

**What B is** (§5): the Magician to A's Sage. Second person, forward motion, one em dash per beat, warm where A is cool. Its stated risk is that it "can tip into promise if a line lacks a fact behind it", so every line below is followed by the fact it rests on. If the fact is wrong, the line goes.

**The rules both variants obey:** active voice, second person for the reader, first person plural for Inflexions, British spelling, title case for headings, no empty superlatives. Added for this draft, per the brief: **no time-anchored claims** — no years served, no "same team since", no counting from a founding date.

**Scope:** headlines, leads and calls to action only. Programme descriptions, curricula, module titles and the `academy/data.ts` catalogue are untouched and are not up for rewrite here.

## 5. What is not drafted, and why

- **Programme titles, subtitles, descriptions, curricula and module lists.** Out of scope by the brief, and they are catalogue data in `academy/data.ts`, not voice.
- **The four domain `description` fields.** They are used as catalogue text in card entries, where A's precision is the right register.
- **The pillar pages, `/services`, `/about`, `/case-study` and `/resources`.** `COPY_DECK.md` §11 puts those in A. They keep their current copy.
